## Resume, Progress, and Webcam Capture

### Resume + Progress
Use `--resume` to skip already-encoded frames; lightweight progress lines are printed by default.

Example:
  python3 scripts/pxrom.py encode tinycore.iso \
    --out artifacts/tinycore_pxrom_fast \
    --tiles 128x72 --tile-size 8 \
    --jobs 4 --compress-level 1 --resume

### Webcam Capture (screen-only)
Capture PxROM frames from a live display via webcam and reconstruct the payload.

  python3 scripts/pxrom_cam_decode.py \
    --out artifacts/tinycore_cam.iso \
    --tiles 128x72 --camera 0 --interval-ms 500 --show

Controls: SPACE/ENTER capture, A auto toggle, Q/ESC quit.
For robustness (angle/keystone/lighting), next steps: fiducials + homography, gamma handling, ECC.
